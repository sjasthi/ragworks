[Use Case1] Who Owns It?

In many big organizations, people spend time in trying to figure out "Who owns <something>?". For example, <something> can be a computer or a software component or a document artifact or a project repo, etc.
If you are given an excel file of such ownership data, how can we build a chat bot that answers the questions on ownership?

For example, (1) Who owns project ABCD? (2) Who owns ABCD Project? (3) Who owns ABCD?  (4) What are all components owned by Jane Doe?  (5) Which team owns the most number of projects?

So, the chat bot answers any question related to the ownership.


[Use Case 2]  Do I have it? Did I cover it?

People manage list of <somethings>. It can be a list of people (contacts) or list of movies they watched or books they read or placed they visited.

They give that list (can be an excel having multile columns or a SQL file reflecting a DB table or Database connection) to the AI Agent.
And then they ask the question to see whether they already covered it or not.

For example, the questions can be:
- Did I read "Roots" novel?  Is Ulysses in my list?
- is Jane Doe in my contacts? What is the mobile of John Doe?
